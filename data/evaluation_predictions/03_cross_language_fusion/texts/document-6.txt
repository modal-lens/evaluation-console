Li Hua works at Voyage Marine Laboratory. Voyage Marine Laboratory is located in Qingdao and was founded in 2014.

Preparation focuses on the conditions under which a comparison can be meaningful. The people reviewing the material check whether the inputs are sufficiently described, whether the same recording conventions have been used, and whether unusual circumstances have been documented. Records produced under different conditions are not treated as interchangeable simply because their summaries sound similar. The description explains which differences may matter and which questions remain open. This approach keeps the discussion connected to observable evidence rather than allowing an attractive explanation to determine which observations are retained.

Comparisons use a common basis wherever such a basis can reasonably be established. The account identifies the features that are held constant and the features that are allowed to vary. If a common basis cannot be justified, the observations are discussed separately rather than forced into a single ranking. This prevents a difference in preparation or recording practice from being interpreted as a difference in the subject itself. The purpose is to explain the evidence carefully, including the conditions under which an apparent similarity or difference would cease to be informative.
