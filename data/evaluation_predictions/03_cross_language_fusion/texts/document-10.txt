Liu Yang works at Sea River Archives. Sea River Archives is located in Xiamen and was founded in 2018.

Preparation focuses on the conditions under which a comparison can be meaningful. The people reviewing the material check whether the inputs are sufficiently described, whether the same recording conventions have been used, and whether unusual circumstances have been documented. Records produced under different conditions are not treated as interchangeable simply because their summaries sound similar. The description explains which differences may matter and which questions remain open. This approach keeps the discussion connected to observable evidence rather than allowing an attractive explanation to determine which observations are retained.

Quality assessment considers completeness, clarity, and the limits of interpretation together. Completeness concerns whether important observations and relevant exceptions have been retained. Clarity concerns whether another reader can follow the explanation without guessing the missing context. Interpretive limits concern whether the conclusions go beyond what the observations can support. A record can satisfy one of these requirements while falling short on another, so a single favorable summary cannot replace a detailed assessment. The narrative explains the relevant strengths and remaining weaknesses rather than collapsing everything into an unexplained overall judgment.
